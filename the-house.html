The House

A small atelier in the 6th

Three hundred bottles per batch. No more, no less. Composed by hand in the rue de Seine, since 2019.

Each composition begins as a phrase scribbled on the back of an envelope — a memory of citrus burnt by the August sun, or the smell of wet stone in the cloister of an empty church. The phrase is lived with, sometimes for months, before it ever reaches a vial.

The atelier is small. There are four of us — a perfumer, two apprentices, and the proprietor who answers your letters. We work above a stationer's shop in the rue de Seine, in a room that smells permanently of vetiver and old paper. The window faces north and the light is constant.

We make small batches, by hand, in Paris. The formula is the small part. The patience is the rest.

Every fragrance in the collection is composed in batches of no more than three hundred bottles. When a batch is exhausted, the composition rests. Some return the following year. Some do not return at all.

We use a small set of materials, sourced from suppliers we know by name. The bergamot comes from a single grove in Calabria. The iris is from Tuscany. The vetiver is from Haiti. The oud is wild, certified, and we use very little of it.

If you would like to visit the atelier, you may. Write to us and we will reply, usually within two working days. There is no shop, but there is a small consultation room with three chairs and a north-facing window. Appointments are by correspondence only.
